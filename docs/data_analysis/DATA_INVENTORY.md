## Critical findings (cross-cutting)

### Most valuable single file
**`drive_sync/swiss_law/v7_pool_recall_089_and_per_query/anchor_funnel_val001_v7/snapshot/corpus_snapshot.json.gz`** (110 MB) — the v7.5 multi-query recall-0.89 pool. Every downstream experiment (cascade dossier, reranker shootout, precision v1) reads from this. Section 5 §B.

### Toxic data — delete before any judge re-run
**`cache_endgame/judge/*` (1788 verdict JSONs, ~860 KB)** — 87% rubber-stamp YES from the broken endgame run. Documented in endgame §4.3. Section 5 §A.

### Build-script bug producing 5.5 GB of redundant data
**`artifacts/court_authority_cards_chunkNN_chunkNN.jsonl`** (10 files, ~5.5 GB total) — accidentally generated by `scripts/build_pipeline/build_court_authority_cards.py:1238-1241` unconditionally appending `_chunkNN` to output stem when `--chunk-index` is set. Re-running with an already-`_chunkNN` stem doubles the suffix. Bodies are method-tag v4 — pure waste. Section 2.

### Stale Drive mirror
**`drive_sync/swiss_law/llm_enrichment_jsonl_checkpoints/law_llm_descriptors_0000000_all.jsonl`** — byte-identical to local `.bak`, NOT the current `_all.jsonl`. Mirror is out of date. Section 3.

### Files documented as wrong in briefs (agent caught + corrected)
- `cache_endgame/query_embeddings.npy` — actually `(20, 4096) fp32`, not 10 vectors. Keys JSON lists 20 hashed IDs. Section 4 §C.
- `embeddings.f16` (10 GB Omnilex) — absent locally; only the 4 sidecar .npy files (`doc_ids.npy`, `case_doc_ids.npy`, `case_mask.npy`, `statute_doc_ids.npy`) were downloaded. Section 4 §E.
- `sqlite_probe.sqlite` lives under `drive_sync/swiss_law/colab_data_insights_mirror/`, NOT under `artifacts/`. Section 4 §B.
