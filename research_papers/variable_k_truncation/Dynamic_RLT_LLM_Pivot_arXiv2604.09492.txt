Figure 3: This diagram shows the generation of a reference-document pivot () for the Query () and a retrieved ranked list
of documents from a first-stage retriever (e.g., BM25 and SPLADE [8, 29]). The utility of this pivot across pointwise, pairwise
(PSI-Rank) and listwise (SNOW, VS-Sliding, and GPTD-Part) configurations for improving ranking performance and efficiency
is illustrated. For pointwise and pairwise methods, we use the pivot for adaptive RLT, requiring only a single inference to
calculate the pivot score using the base-retrieval method (PSI-Rank). All documents with a score above the pivot (green coloured)
are selected for reranking. For listwise setups, we rerank the entire ranked list without truncation. We propose three optimized
reranking methods that use the pivot to make informed decisions during the ranking. SNOW uses a parallel non-overlapping
window with the pivot enabling document movement across windows. VS-Sliding utilizes the pivot to promote variable stride.
It takes long strides at the bottom and gradually takes smaller strides when ranking top positions, where the density of relevant
documents is higher. Finally, we show GPTD-Part, an adaptation of TD-Part (an efficient listwise reranking framework), and
show that our generated pivot further improves its performance.
